ReelCount Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

ReelCount (“ReelCount”, “we”, “us”) is an Android app that counts the reels and short videos you watch, so you can see your habits and cut down if you want to. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Your reel counts and history stay on your phone. We have no servers that receive them, and there is no ReelCount account.
  • ReelCount never reads your messages, comments, captions, usernames, videos, or anything you type.
  • The free version shows non-personalised ads from Google AdMob. Purchases of ReelCount Pro go through Google Play and RevenueCat. These services receive limited device information, described below.
  • We do not sell your information.

1. How counting works (Accessibility service)

ReelCount counts reels using Android’s Accessibility service. Before you turn it on, the app shows a screen explaining what it does and asks for your agreement. Android then asks you to switch it on yourself in Accessibility settings.

Where it works

The service only receives events from these apps: Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Snapchat, Moj, Josh and TikTok. It does not receive events from any other app.

What it uses

  • When a reels or shorts screen opens or closes.
  • When you scroll to a new reel.
  • Technical layout details needed to recognise a reels screen: view identifiers, view types, scroll positions and on-screen sizes.

What it never reads or collects

  • Messages, comments, captions, usernames or profile details.
  • Videos, images or other media.
  • Anything you type, including passwords.

What the service notices is turned into a count on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. The service is also used to draw the on-screen features you choose to turn on, such as the floating counter and eye-rest break screens.

You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility → ReelCount. Counting stops straight away.

2. Information stored on your phone

ReelCount stores the following only in its private storage on your device:

  • Reel counts, with the time and which supported app they came from.
  • Your daily goal, limits, reminder and eye-rest settings, and whether you took or snoozed a break.
  • Streaks, badges and other stats calculated from your counts.
  • App settings such as theme and language, and whether ReelCount Pro is active.

Reminders and summaries are created on your phone as local notifications. The home-screen widget shows your counts from the same on-device data. App backup is turned off, so this information is not copied to Google Drive backups.

To delete it, go to Android Settings → Apps → ReelCount → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

3. Ads in the free version (Google AdMob)

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that unlock a Pro feature for 24 hours. ReelCount Pro users see no ads.

  • ReelCount only requests non-personalised ads, and the app does not use your Android advertising ID.
  • To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your IP address (which can indicate your general location), device and app information (such as device model, Android version and app version), app-specific device identifiers (such as the app set ID), ad interactions, and diagnostic information.
  • Google’s handling of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. ReelCount Pro purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

  • Payments are processed by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We use RevenueCat to check and restore your Pro subscription. RevenueCat receives purchase details (such as the product, purchase token, purchase dates and price), a random app user ID created by RevenueCat that is not linked to your name or email, device and app information (such as Android version, app version and store country), and your IP address. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • This information is used only to unlock Pro, restore purchases and keep subscription records.

5. Inviting friends

If you invite a friend, ReelCount opens Android’s share sheet. We do not receive the message or who you shared it with.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell your information or share it for advertising beyond the non-personalised ads described above.

7. Children

ReelCount is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Security and retention

Your on-device data is protected by Android’s app sandbox and stays until you clear it or uninstall the app. Purchase records held by Google Play and RevenueCat are kept as long as needed to provide Pro and meet legal requirements. Ad data is kept according to Google’s policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Because your counts never leave your phone, you can delete them yourself as described in section 2. For anything held by RevenueCat on our behalf, email us and we will act on your request within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will tell you in the app.
