Delete your ReelCount data

For the ReelCount Android app · Last updated 25 October 2026

In short

  • There is no ReelCount account. Your reel counts and history live only on your phone, and you can delete them yourself at any time.
  • If you bought ReelCount Pro, email us and we will delete your purchase record held by RevenueCat within 30 days.

1. Delete the data on your phone

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → ReelCount.
  2. Tap Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall ReelCount.

This deletes, straight away and for good: your reel counts and history, daily goal, limits, reminder and eye-rest settings and break history, streaks, badges and stats, and app settings. This data is never sent to us, so there is no copy anywhere else, and app backup is turned off.

2. Request deletion of your Pro purchase record

If you bought ReelCount Pro, RevenueCat keeps a record of it for us so your purchase can be checked and restored.

  1. If your subscription is still active, cancel it first in the Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions. Otherwise the app creates a new record the next time it checks your subscription.
  2. Email cvpdfeditor@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my ReelCount data”.
  3. Include your Google Play order ID. It starts with “GPA.” and is in the receipt email Google Play sent you. It lets us find your record, because we never ask for your name or email in the app.

Within 30 days we delete your RevenueCat record: the random app user ID, purchase details (product, purchase token, dates and price), device and app information, and IP address. We reply by email when it is done.

3. What is kept

  • Google Play payment records. Google processes payments and keeps its own order records under the Google Privacy Policy. We cannot delete these; you can manage them in your Google Account.
  • Ad data. The free version shows non-personalised ads from Google AdMob. We do not receive or store this data; Google keeps it according to its own policies.
  • Records the law requires. We or RevenueCat may keep a minimal purchase record if a law (for example tax law) requires it, only for as long as required.

Read the full ReelCount Privacy Policy for more detail.
